## Educational content

The original `Svaly.json` is unchanged, including its 64 stable IDs, names, and development notes. The restored Preview uses the original names and a separate set of brief, English descriptions for the 31 active meshes. The new descriptions cover basic anatomical actions. [OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology, muscles of the pectoral girdle and upper limbs](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/11-5-muscles-of-the-pectoral-girdle-and-upper-limbs)

All ten answer keys and the original illustration order are retained. The quiz reports answers against those source keys. Verify these cases with the original author or an anatomy educator before instructional release.

- Scenario 9 describes a supinated forearm. Its original key has no dedicated supinator ID.
- Scenario 10 describes a flexed wrist. Its original key includes finger flexors and pronators, while the two dedicated wrist flexors occur in scenario 4 and scenario 9.

The original study labels also include legacy spelling and naming variants. Catalog IDs are mesh bindings, so update names by ID if reviewed copy becomes available.
